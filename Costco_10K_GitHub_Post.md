# Costco: FY2025 10-K financial snapshot

This post uses Costco Wholesale Corporation's latest Form 10-K, for the fiscal year ended **August 31, 2025**. Dollar amounts are in millions unless noted otherwise.

## Operating performance

| Metric | FY2025 | FY2024 | FY2023 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Net sales | $269,912 | $249,625 | $237,710 |
| Net cash provided by operating activities | $13,335 | $11,339 | $11,068 |
| Additions to property and equipment | $5,498 | $4,710 | $4,323 |
| Operating income | $10,383 | $9,285 | $8,114 |
| Net income | $8,099 | $7,367 | $6,292 |

Costco reported **8% net-sales growth** in FY2025. Its FY2023-to-FY2025 net-sales compound annual growth rate was **6.56%**, calculated from $237.710 billion to $269.912 billion.

## Free-cash-flow starting point

A simple FCFF starting point derived from the 10-K is:

```text
Operating cash flow                         $13,335.0
+ after-tax interest expense                   115.3
- additions to property and equipment        5,498.0
= starting FCFF                              $7,952.3
```

The after-tax interest add-back uses FY2025 interest expense of $154 million and Costco's 25.1% effective tax rate:

```text
$154.0 × (1 − 25.1%) = $115.3 million
```
